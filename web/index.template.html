Sources & methodology

EPA/100 plays = EPA/play × 100. Ranks are unchanged. Color shows league rank for the selected unit and metric; lower defensive EPA allowed is better.

The default follows the date you visit, with NFL weeks rolling over Tuesday at midnight America/New_York. Before the season, Week 1 is shown; after the season, the final published week is shown. Kickoff times and game-day headings use your browser’s timezone, including daylight saving time. A shared link with a week selected keeps that week.

This is a bundled schedule snapshot. Flex scheduling and other changes appear after the schedule is refreshed and the site rebuilt. Games with an unannounced time are labeled “Kickoff TBD.” Playoff rounds will be included when the source publishes them. EPA advances on Tuesdays after source data is complete. Matchups use the latest saved snapshot ending before that game’s week; earlier weeks without a saved snapshot leave EPA unavailable to avoid hindsight. The special teams possession/opponent columns are not fixed kicking/returning units; kickoff possession belongs to the receiver, while punt possession belongs to the punter. Green means stronger league rank, not a positive EPA sign. Betting quotes are saved pregame snapshots from FanDuel via The Odds API. Weekly opening is the Sunday-midnight Pacific baseline before that NFL week. A quote is only accepted for that baseline if its provider timestamp is at or before midnight and the opening job captures it within 15 minutes. Delayed or missing quotes leave opening unavailable. The scheduled latest refreshes target 4 a.m. and 4 p.m. Pacific; GitHub scheduling is best effort and may be delayed. Missing markets are not filled from other books.

How to read these numbers

Offense and possession-side special teams: higher is better. Defense and opponent-side special teams: lower EPA allowed is better. Defensive values keep their raw sign. The plot always puts the best team on the right, so the numeric axis reverses for defense and opponent-side special teams.

Total EPA sums qualifying plays. EPA/100 plays divides that total by qualifying plays with non-null EPA and multiplies by 100. EPA/game divides it by all completed team games through the labeled cutoff, accounting for byes. Pass and rush follow nflverse flags; special teams includes punts, kickoffs, field goals, and extra points. No garbage-time or opponent adjustments. Special teams possession total minus opponent total equals net special teams EPA.
