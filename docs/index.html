How to read these numbers

Offense and possession-side special teams: higher is better. Defense and opponent-side special teams: lower EPA allowed is better. Defensive values keep their raw sign. The plot always puts the best team on the left, so the numeric axis reverses for offense.

Total EPA sums qualifying plays. EPA/play divides that total by qualifying plays with non-null EPA. EPA/game divides it by all completed team games through Week 5, accounting for byes. Pass and rush follow nflverse flags; special teams includes punts, kickoffs, field goals, and extra points. No garbage-time or opponent adjustments. Special teams possession total minus opponent total equals net special teams EPA.
